The Core Problem & Breakthrough Solution

From Weather Information
To Weather Decisions.

Weather data is fragmented across dozens of portals—radars, satellites, river gauges, and ocean models. Traditional apps simply dump passive forecasts like "18°C, 70% rain". WeatherGPT understands what you are trying to do and orchestrates real-time meteorological tools into actionable domain advice.

💡
“ChatGPT tells you the weather.
WeatherGPT tells you what the weather means for what you are trying to do.”
The Fundamental Paradigm Shift · SIH Problem Statement #SIH26068

Action: Reschedule departure to 7:15 AM to arrive at the ghat section post-storm, or divert via the Kiratpur-Nerchowk bypass.”
